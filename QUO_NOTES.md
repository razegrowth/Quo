# What's new in Quo

Newest first. Each version starts with its own heading, `## 0.1.0 · 20 November 2026`, and the paragraphs under it. The first paragraph of the newest is the line under the version in Settings › About and the release's notes on GitHub; all of them are in Settings › About › What's New, above Search's own.

## 0.7.0 · 23 November 2026

A Library past your first space, Apple Intelligence on your tabs and links, strong passwords and cards at checkout, and your tabs and history on all your devices.

Swipe right from your first space and the Library comes in with your fingers: Downloads first, then Media, Spaces, Bookmarks and Archived Tabs. The button at the column's foot opens it too.

Hold Shift over a link and a card tells you what the page says, summarised on your Mac by Apple Intelligence, private pages you're signed in to included. Tidy Tabs sorts a space's loose tabs into named groups, and Shorter tab names (Settings › Tabs) turns long titles into a few words.

Sign-up forms offer a strong password, and checkouts offer your cards after Touch ID: add them in Settings › Passwords. Send a page to your iPhone or another Mac from a tab's menu or Share, and your history follows you between devices while sync is on. The page in front is offered through Handoff, and View › Listen to Page reads an article aloud.

Perplexity and ChatGPT are among the search engines, right-clicking the column brings up the space's menu, and download rows show small icons instead of buttons.

## 0.6.0 · 22 November 2026

A simpler Settings, motion that answers at once, and a calmer ⌘T.

Each page of Settings now shows what most people come for, with everything else folded under Advanced at the bottom of the page, and the switches and buttons are a size up with Roomy. Find a setting still finds all of them.

Everything moves on a firmer curve: buttons give a little when pressed and come straight back, cards and highlights land without a wobble, and ⌘T and ⌘L open the moment you press them. The page behind ⌘T is dimmed and very slightly blurred instead of washed out in white.

With Icons only, the window's three buttons sit in a row at the start of the address bar again instead of stacked up the column. Site icons are on from the start. Bringing Arc over during the first run brings it straight in, without asking a second time.

Quo now counts how many people use it: once a day it tells TelemetryDeck it was opened, with its version and your Mac's model and macOS, as a random number hashed on your Mac. Never what you browse. Turn it off in Settings › Privacy.

## 0.5.0 · 21 November 2026

Your open tabs follow you: what's open on your other Macs comes up in ⌘T, ready to pick up where you left off.

Press ⌘T and the pages you were last looking at on another Mac sit under your own open tabs, with the Mac's name beside them; start typing and the ones that match come up too. Return opens one here in a new tab. Each Mac says what it has open through your own iCloud, end to end encrypted, within half a minute of a change and the moment you switch away from it, and never a private tab, a pin, or a tab an agent is working in. It's ready for Quo for iPhone too: its tabs will show up the same way.

## 0.4.0 · 21 November 2026

Pinned Calendar and Gmail that tell you what's coming, as Arc's did, plus the fixes and import support from Search 1.0.16.

A pinned Google Calendar counts down to your next meeting in the corner of its pin, 30m and then now, and offers Join over the foot of the column a few minutes before a call. Pinned Gmail shows a small red dot while you have unread mail. Rest the pointer on either and a card comes up beside it: your next meetings with Join, or your latest email as Gmail's notification said it, with a + to write a new one. Your meetings are read from the calendars on this Mac once you allow it, and nothing leaves it.

From Search 1.0.16: no more crash on macOS 27.2, the ad blocker is on from the very first page after launch, and extensions like Zotero and Stylus work, with Settings saying why one couldn't start. Bring Things Over now brings Zen's spaces, open tabs from Firefox and Zen, Safari directly when macOS allows it, and more browsers, including Thorium, ChatGPT Atlas and Orion.

A tab's close button matches ⌘T's Switch to Tab arrow, pinned icons are a touch smaller, and clicking the tab you're on to edit its address always works.

## 0.3.0 · 20 November 2026

A new first run that opens with a flourish, every space at the column's foot as in Arc, and a smoother way to make a space.

The onboarding opens on black with the mark lighting up out of it, then takes on the colour you pick for your space as you pick it, on dark glass cards that read at a glance. Bringing things over is calmer: nothing switched on that there's nothing of, Arc's spaces as one of Arc's choices, and a card that says which extension is being installed and what it's waiting on.

The foot of the column shows every space as a row of icons, the one you're in lit, with + for another, a size up with Roomy. Making a space fades the tabs away and brings its card in a part at a time. Bookmarks rise from the foot as a card, and the Colour… picker and the new space card are a size up with Roomy.

## 0.2.0 · 20 November 2026

The small window for links as Arc's, a colour wheel for spaces, an empty tab that's your space's home, ⌘T with your open tabs, and extensions that slow pages down offered On click.

The small window for links is larger, springs open, and flies into its place when you keep it, with Open in the space you choose (⌘O). A space's colour is picked on a wheel, as one colour or a gradient, with film grain. An empty tab shows the space's name, the sites you visit most and the tabs you closed last, and ⌃1–9 or ⌥⌘← and → move between spaces.

⌘T lists the tabs you were last in, each a Switch to Tab away, puts the open tabs that match first as you type, and offers the menus' commands too. Right-click an address you've selected to go to it.

Bring Things Over now finds Chrome however it's installed, and brings every profile and its extensions. An extension that takes more than two seconds of the pages' time in a quarter of an hour is offered On click in a card at the side, with Don't Ask Again; tools like SEO and font inspectors start On click when installed.

The column lines up with the page, menus show their icons again, Roomy tabs are a little larger, and hovering in pages is smoother.

## 0.1.0 · 20 November 2026

Quo's first release of its own: sync through iCloud, end to end encrypted, an onboarding to set Quo up your way, and updates that arrive on their own from now on.
